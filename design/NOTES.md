# Design notes — simplicityscaling.com

Visual direction: match [simplicitymedia.com](https://www.simplicitymedia.com) chrome so this owned-domain funnel reads as the same company. Copy stays `content/lp-copy-v1.md`. PRD §9 still applies (no collage, no C08 imagery, no homepage CTA).

## Brand tokens (from the live site, Sep 2026)

- **Sans:** Sora (`/fonts/sora-variable.woff2`) — body and UI
- **Display / accent:** Space Grotesk (`/fonts/space-grotesk-variable.woff2`) — headlines, CTAs, eyebrows, thesis
- **Logo:** `/images/simplicity-logo.webp` (header, `h-12` / `lg:h-14`)
- **Mark:** four diagonal spokes, `#2f8dcd` + `#ff884d` (`/images/simplicity-logo.svg`)
- **Accent blue:** `#2190DC` (links, badges, focus)
- **Brand orange:** `#ff884d` (logo mark)
- **Ink:** `#18181b` / `#09090b`
- **Paper:** `#fafafa` with white cards, `#e4e4e7` rules
- **CTA:** black pill, Space Grotesk bold, arrow (`btn-primary`)
- **Eyebrow:** white frosted pill, uppercase Space Grotesk
- **Theme color:** `#0c1033`
- **Footer:** `neutral-950` with `#2190DC` / `#d4621a` glow blobs and oversized “simplicity” wordmark

## Do

- White nav, official wordmark only (no campaign subtitle in the header)
- Tight Space Grotesk headlines, Sora body
- Proof as typeset facts
- Case study as the only simplicitymedia.com CTA

## Do not

- Warm cream / bronze “memo” palette (that was a draft direction, not their site)
- Stock growth collage, spokesperson photos, jingle art
- Primary CTAs to the corporate homepage
- Rewrite approved LP copy to sound more “on brand”
