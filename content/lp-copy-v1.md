## Four moves. One accountable loop.

The Optima work reads as a feedback loop: prove response, build recognition, read demand, then improve the next buy. Budget scales from that loop. Channel count alone does not.

1. **Prove response** — Start narrow enough to diagnose. The test must produce calls, appointments, enrollments, or orders you trust.
2. **Build recognition** — In high-need, low-trust categories, creative earns credibility while it asks for action.
3. **Read demand** — Inquiries, questions, markets, creatives, branded search, landing behavior, and call quality in one picture.
4. **Improve the next buy** — Send revenue economics back into allocation. Expand capacity with growth.

## Same operating clarity. Different response event.

### Finance and legal

**Response event:** Qualified call → enrollment, application, or signed case.

**First engagement:** Bounded TV / CTV / audio response test, approved creative, eligibility or process pages, source-to-outcome tracking in states you can serve.

### Local consultations

**Response event:** Call or form → booked consultation → completed, paid job or treatment.

**First engagement:** One-market launch or expansion with demonstration creative, service-area pages, cost-per-appointment reporting.

### Products and memberships

**Response event:** Exposure → landing behavior → paid customer or enrollment (repeat / contribution where finance shares it).

**First engagement:** CTV / audio demonstrations, landing variants, holdout or incrementality on one acquisition cohort.
